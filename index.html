A NOTE ON THE NUMBERS

Built for exploration.

Every metric is synthetic, deterministic sample data for demonstrating this app. Values are not official statistics and should not be used for research or decisions.

The 2024 label is an illustrative reference year. The median is unweighted across available countries and territories. Rankings use the selected metric; a higher value is not a universal measure of wellbeing.

Boundaries come from Natural Earth via World Atlas at 1:110m resolution. Small countries may be absent, and boundaries do not imply a position on territorial claims. Gray areas have no sample data.

Use search or Tab and Enter to select a country. Use +, −, and the reset control to explore the map. Download exports all sample metrics as CSV.
